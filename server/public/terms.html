What MessagesBridge does

MessagesBridge lets an AI assistant (e.g. ChatGPT) read and search the Messages (iMessage & SMS) on your own Mac, send a message on your behalf after you confirm it, and read or add Contacts. These operations run locally on your Mac via an agent you install; the hosted relay only forwards each request while it is in flight and does not store your message content. The connector reads first by default, confirms every send with you, and messages one recipient at a time — it does not perform bulk or blast sends.
